Quick Tips

🎯 Use Wildcards

Blank tiles can be any letter. Select how many wildcards you have and find words that use them strategically for high scores.

🏆 Hunt for Bingos

Seven-letter words earn a 50-point bonus. Always check if you can use all your tiles before settling for shorter plays.

📚 Choose Your Dictionary

Collins 2019 for international play, OTCWL for North America, SOWPODS for both. Pick the right one for your game.

How to Use This Tool

Step 1: Enter Your Letters - Type the letters from your Scrabble rack into the search field. You can enter up to 15 letters. Don't worry about order - just enter all available letters.

Step 2: Add Wildcards (Optional) - If you have blank tiles, use the wildcard selector to specify how many (0, 1, or 2). Blank tiles can represent any letter and are incredibly valuable for creating high-scoring words.

Step 3: Set Word Length - Use the slider to choose your preferred word length range, or check "Show all words" to see every possible combination regardless of length.

Step 4: Find Words - Click the "Find Words" button to see all valid words from the Collins 2019 dictionary (default). Results are automatically sorted by score with the highest-scoring options appearing first.

Step 5: Switch Dictionaries (Optional) - After results load, you can select a different dictionary from the dropdown menu. Choose OTCWL 2016 for North American games or SOWPODS for combined British and American words to see how word availability changes between different official Scrabble word lists.
